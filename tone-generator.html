Pick a preset above, or flip any layer’s switch below to build your own sound. Each of the six layers is an independent oscillator with its own waveform, frequency (0.1–20,000 Hz), volume, and stereo position. Every control glides smoothly, so you can reshape the sound while it plays.

🎧 Headphones required. A binaural beat only arises when each ear receives its own tone; over speakers the two tones simply mix in the air and the effect is largely lost.

All sound is synthesized locally with the Web Audio API — no network requests, no account, nothing recorded. This is a listening and relaxation tool, not a medical device: none of these presets diagnose, treat, or cure any condition, and the preset notes above reflect the current state of the evidence. Keep the volume comfortable, especially with headphones, and stop listening if you feel discomfort.
